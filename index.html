Professional Bio

About Me

My name is Zihuan Wang. I have a background in Mechanical Engineering and am expanding my skills in Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics through AIML-500: Foundations of Machine Learning.

I bring a structured, systems-oriented approach to emerging technology. My work focuses on understanding how AI changes products and operations, then translating that change into clear recommendations for decision-makers.

I am especially interested in work at the intersection of engineering, intelligent systems, and business strategy—where technical understanding and practical communication are equally important.

What I Bring

Engineering discipline, AI literacy, competitive research, and the ability to turn complex information into an accessible visual story.
